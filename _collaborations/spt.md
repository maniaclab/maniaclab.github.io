MANIAC Lab operates cyberinfrastructure for the South Pole Telescope (SPT), both at the Pole and in the SPT analysis facilities in Chicago. We provision and maintain the storage and compute systems used by the collaboration, and have supported SPT-3G high-throughput data processing on the Open Science Grid.
