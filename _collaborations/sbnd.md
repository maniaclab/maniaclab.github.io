SBND, the Short-Baseline Neutrino Detector, is a liquid argon time projection chamber on the Booster Neutrino Beam at Fermilab and part of the Short-Baseline Neutrino program, searching for sterile neutrinos and making precision neutrino interaction measurements.

In 2026 MANIAC Lab brought the Pile cluster back into service for the University of Chicago SBND group and its students. Pile runs on a Kubernetes substrate and provides interactive login nodes, HTCondor batch computing, CVMFS access to Fermilab software, shared project storage for ntuples and derived data frames, and a Globus data transfer endpoint for moving datasets from Fermilab. It is an example of extending the Analysis Facility model, built for the LHC, to a neutrino experiment with modest, well-defined needs.
