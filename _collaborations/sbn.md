The Short-Baseline Neutrino (SBN) program at Fermilab comprises three liquid argon time projection chamber detectors, SBND, MicroBooNE and ICARUS, on the Booster Neutrino Beam, searching for sterile neutrinos and making precision neutrino interaction measurements.

MANIAC Lab is working with the University of Chicago SBN group to extend the Analysis Facility model beyond the LHC: Ceph storage for SBN datasets and derived samples, JupyterHub and Dask for interactive analysis, and HTCondor batch for processing, all operated as managed services on Kubernetes.
