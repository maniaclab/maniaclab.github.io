MANIAC Lab operates the Rucio global data management service for the XENON dark matter experiment at the Gran Sasso National Laboratory in Italy. Rucio catalogs XENONnT data and replicates it among storage endpoints in Europe and the United States, so that collaborators can process and analyze it wherever their computing lives.

We work with the UChicago XENON group and the collaboration's computing team on storage endpoints, transfer monitoring and access to the data from analysis systems, including the Research Computing Center at the University of Chicago.
